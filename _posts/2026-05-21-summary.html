GitHub confirms breach of 3,800 repos via malicious VSCode extension ⭐ 9.455125/10

hackernews · Timofeibu · May 20, 13:43 · Discussion

EN

GitHub announced that a malicious Visual Studio Code extension compromised 3,800 repositories, exposing internal code. The breach was discovered after community members reported suspicious activity and raised security concerns. This breach highlights the risk of supply chain attacks in developer tools, affecting thousands of projects and prompting calls for stronger extension security. Developers and organizations will need to reassess trust in third‐party extensions and adopt stricter verification practices. The malicious extension, distributed through the VSCode marketplace, allowed attackers to gain unauthorized access to GitHub's internal repositories, affecting 3,800 repositories. Community members noted that the lack of a robust permission model in VSCode enables such supply chain attacks and urged Microsoft to implement explicit extension permissions.
